Our History

Shokat Ali Public School was established in 2010 with a vision to provide quality education at affordable cost to the children of Pakistan. Starting with just a few classrooms and a handful of students, the school has grown into a well-recognized institution known for academic excellence and character development.

Under the leadership of Director Shokat Ali, the school has continuously upgraded its facilities, curriculum and teaching methods to meet modern educational standards while preserving Islamic and national values.

Our Mission

To provide holistic education that develops intellectual, moral, social and physical abilities of every student, preparing them to be responsible citizens and future leaders of Pakistan.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution recognized for academic excellence, innovation in teaching, and nurturing values that shape outstanding human beings.

Our Objectives

Deliver quality education, promote critical thinking, instill moral values, encourage co-curricular activities, and build a strong partnership with parents.

Principal / Director Message

Education is the most powerful tool we can use to change the world. At Shokat Ali Public School, we are committed to providing an environment where every child feels valued, challenged and inspired.

Our dedicated team of educators works with passion to ensure that learning is engaging, meaningful and relevant. We focus not only on academic achievement but also on developing good character, leadership skills and a sense of social responsibility.

We look forward to partnering with you in your child's educational journey.
